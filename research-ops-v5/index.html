Research Ops V5

Research Ops V5 is a personal assistant used by its owner to organize PhD opportunities, applications, academic correspondence, and deadlines.

The Google integration reads the owner's Gmail, selected Google Sheets application records, and Google Calendar events to identify academic actions and prepare private summaries. It uses deterministic project rules to classify evidence and maintain a deduplicated action history.

What this integration does

  • Reads academic email details to identify meaningful replies and requests. For a likely interview message, it may temporarily inspect text-only content when the message has no attachments.
  • Reads selected application and deadline fields from Google Sheets, and event timing details from Google Calendar.
  • Produces private action summaries and briefings for the account owner.
  • Supports optional Telegram alerts to the owner's configured private chat after separate activation approval. Alerts use a short event category, a generic required action, and an opaque reference. See the Privacy Policy for sharing details.

What it does not do

  • It does not send or draft email, change Gmail labels, edit Google Sheets, create or modify Calendar events, or submit applications.
  • It does not download email attachments or send Google account data to an AI model provider.
  • It is a single-owner personal tool with no public sign-up or multi-user workflow.
